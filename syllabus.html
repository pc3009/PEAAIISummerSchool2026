The 5-Day Journey

Five Days. Five Big Questions.

Each day introduces a new dimension of artificial intelligence, while moving students closer to their final project.

01

What Is Intelligence? slides

+ How to Do Research

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand what AI is — and isn't
  • Compare human vs. machine intelligence
  • Learn how to think like a researcher from scratch

🧠 Mini-Lectures

  • What is Intelligence?
  • How neural networks learn
  • Word embeddings as "vibes in math space"
  • Why large models feel intelligent

🔬 Special Session: From Zero to One — How to Start Research

Designed specifically for students with no prior research experience.

  • What research really is
  • Turn curiosity into meaningful questions
  • Why do AI models hallucinate?
  • Why do models show bias?
  • How to formulate a strong research question
  • How to find and use resources
  • How to design a simple experiment
  • How to analyze results and draw conclusions

🌟 By the end: each student develops one original AI-related research question.

🧪 Hands-On Lab Day_1_PEAAII_MNIST, Day_1_PEAAII_Intro_to_Python

  • Embedding Playground — explore analogies such as "king - man + woman = ?"
  • Build a Tiny Neural Network — visual or beginner-friendly Python demo
  • Vibe Classifier — classify text as happy, academic, chaotic, etc.
🏆 Day 1 Deliverable: A clearly defined research question + initial idea for the final project.
02

Inside the Black Box slides

Mechanistic Interpretability

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand transformers and attention intuitively
  • Learn what features and circuits are
  • Explore how we interpret model behavior

🧠 Mini-Lectures

  • How transformers process text
  • Attention heads as circuits
  • Neuron activations and features
  • The "grokking" phenomenon

🧪 Hands-On Lab

  • 🔥 Attention Heatmap Visualization
  • 🧠 Neuron Exploration
  • 🤖 Tiny Transformer Demo
🏆 Day 2 Deliverable: Add an interpretability component to the project — for example, explaining how the model makes decisions.
03

Consciousness, Minds & Theories of Thought slides

Can Machines Really Think?

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Explore major theories of consciousness
  • Compare AI representations with human cognition
  • Understand limitations of current AI systems

🧠 Mini-Lectures

  • Global Workspace Theory
  • Integrated Information Theory
  • Representation vs. experience
  • Can machines be conscious?

🧪 Hands-On Lab

  • 🧩 Chinese Room Simulation
  • 🗺️ Concept Embedding Exploration
  • 👁️ Self-Awareness Tests using prompt-based experiments
🏆 Day 3 Deliverable: Add a conceptual or philosophical dimension to the project — useful for essays, interviews, and interdisciplinary thinking.
04

Vibes, Aesthetics & Creative AI slides

Turn AI Into a Creative Partner

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand generative AI for text and images
  • Explore latent space and style
  • Create meaningful AI-generated artifacts

🧠 Mini-Lectures

  • Latent space as a "vibe landscape"
  • Style transfer techniques
  • Ethics and safety in generative AI
  • Multi-agent creativity systems

🎨 Hands-On Lab

  • 🖼️ Image Style Mixing
  • ✨ Text "Vibe Transfer"
  • 📚 Multi-Agent Story Creation
🏆 Day 4 Deliverable: Build the core output of the final project — visual, narrative, or interactive.
05

Build, Polish & Present slides

Turn Your Idea Into a Finished Project

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Finalize and refine the project
  • Learn how to present like a researcher
  • Understand responsible AI development

🧠 Mini-Lectures

  • AI alignment basics
  • Evaluating AI behavior
  • How to present technical + creative work
🏆 Day 5 Deliverable: A polished AI project, research-style write-up, and final presentation.
